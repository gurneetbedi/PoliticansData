The Tenth Nitish Kumar ministry was the Council of Ministers in Bihar Legislative Assembly headed by Chief Minister Nitish Kumar. He was sworn in as the Chief Minister of Bihar on 20 November 2025[2][3] along with 26 Ministers.

The portfolios were allotted on 21 November 2025, in which BJP held the largest share in the cabinet with 14 ministerial berths, JD(U) with 9, LJP (Ram Vilas) with 2 berths, HAM and RLM with 1 berth each.[4][5][6][7]
